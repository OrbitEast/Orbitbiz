From customer to collection

A connected sales workflow can preserve the relationship between a customer, quotation, invoice and payment. That continuity gives each later step useful context instead of requiring the same information to be entered again.

Less re-entry, more continuity

The goal is simple: enter business information once and let connected workflows carry it forward. OrbitBiz is built around that principle across customer, sales, inventory and finance records.
