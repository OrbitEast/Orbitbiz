What finance connects to

Customer payments can connect to invoices and receipts. Supplier payments can connect to purchasing activity. Expenses and reconciliation add another layer of financial visibility to the operational records.

Finance connected to operations

Financial records become more useful when they connect back to customers, invoices, suppliers and purchases. OrbitBiz is designed to keep those relationships visible for reporting and review.
