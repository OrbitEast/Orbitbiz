Why stock history matters

A quantity alone does not explain an inventory change. Stock movements can provide context for incoming purchases, outgoing sales and other operational changes.

Inventory connected to buying and selling

Sales can affect stock, purchasing can bring stock in, and warehouse records can show where quantities are held. This connected model is designed to reduce duplicate inventory work.
