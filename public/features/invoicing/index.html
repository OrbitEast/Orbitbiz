INVOICING · BILLING · PDF

Create an invoice.
Then send it.

OrbitBiz gives you one clear invoice workflow: select a customer, pick products or services from your inventory, or type a custom line yourself. Enter quantity, price, discount and tax, generate the invoice, and turn it into a professional PDF.

Inventory or custom

THE INVOICE BUILDER

Everything needed to make the document is in one place.

You do not need to leave the invoice workflow just to add a product. Inventory items can be selected directly, while custom lines let you invoice services, one-off work or anything not stored in the catalogue.

Inventory connectedSelect catalogue items and reuse their saved selling price and tax rate.
Custom linesType your own description, quantity and price whenever a catalogue item is not appropriate.
PDF + WhatsAppAfter creation, choose a professional PDF template, download it or share it through the supported WhatsApp flow.

One invoice, connected to the workspace

Creating an invoice does not open a separate invoicing application. It uses the same OrbitBiz workspace, customer records, catalogue, payments and reporting data, so the invoice remains part of the business record.
