What an invoicing workflow can connect

A quotation can lead into an invoice. An invoice can connect to payment activity and a receipt. Customer records and reports can then use that transaction history for follow-up and review.

From sale to payment

OrbitBiz is designed so invoicing is part of the sales workflow. Quotations can become invoices, invoice status can reflect payment activity, and receipts can be connected back to the relevant invoice.
