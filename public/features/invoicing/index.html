Why simple invoicing matters

An invoice is one of the most common business documents, but managing invoices can become difficult when information is spread across spreadsheets, messages and separate tools. OrbitBiz aims to make the first step straightforward while keeping the underlying records ready for connected workflows.

How the connected workflow works

A quotation can lead into an invoice. An invoice can connect to payment activity and a receipt. Customer records and reports can then use that transaction history for follow-up and review. The purpose is not to make every business start with a complex system; it is to let a simple task grow into a connected workflow when needed.

Who OrbitBiz is being built for

The current direction focuses on small business owners, startups, service businesses and similar teams that need practical invoicing and business management without unnecessary complexity.

Planned invoice improvements

Future product directions include more business-type-specific invoice generation, stronger printing support and easier digital document sharing through channels such as email or mobile messaging. These capabilities are planned and should not be treated as currently available until released.
