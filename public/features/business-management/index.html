BUSINESS MANAGEMENT

One workspace for the whole business.

OrbitBiz brings day-to-day business operations into a connected workspace, so customers, sales, stock, purchases, payments and reporting can work from the same business data.

01

Connected operations

Move from customer activity to quotations, invoices, payments and reports without rebuilding the same information.

02

Business records

Keep customers, suppliers, products, warehouses and financial activity organised in one system.

03

Clear visibility

Use dashboards and reports to understand activity, receivables, expenses, stock and business performance.

Built around connected workflows

OrbitBiz is designed around relationships between business records rather than isolated tools. A customer can connect to sales activity; a sale can connect to an invoice and payment; purchasing can connect suppliers, purchase orders and stock; reports can use the resulting business activity.
