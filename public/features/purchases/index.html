Why purchasing needs continuity

A supplier record, purchase order and received stock represent different stages of the same operational process. Keeping them connected can make procurement easier to follow and review.

From supplier to warehouse

Purchasing is connected to inventory and finance: a purchase order can become a received purchase, stock can be updated, and supplier payments can be recorded as part of the same business picture.
