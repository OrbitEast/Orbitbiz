Why customer context matters

Customer information is more useful when it can explain the transactions around it. A structured customer record can provide context for follow-ups, quotations, invoices, payment history and future sales activity.

A CRM that belongs to the business

Rather than treating CRM as a separate island, OrbitBiz connects customer information with operational records. That makes customer context useful when you are selling, invoicing, following up or reviewing reports.
