Expense tracking for small businesses

Expense tracking means maintaining a reliable record of money spent to operate the business. The basic process is simple, but consistency matters because expense records feed into financial review and reporting.

What should an expense record contain?

Useful fields can include the date, supplier or payee, description, category, amount, payment method and any supporting reference. Depending on the business, tax information or attachments may also be relevant.

Why categories help

Categories turn a long list of transactions into information that can be reviewed. A business may group expenses into areas such as rent, utilities, supplies, transport, software or professional services. Categories should reflect the business's reporting needs and applicable accounting practice.

Record expenses consistently

Entering expenses immediately or on a regular schedule reduces forgotten transactions. Avoid relying only on bank statements to reconstruct business activity later, because a bank transaction may not contain enough context about why the money was spent.

Connect expenses to finance

Expense records become more useful when they are part of the same financial workflow as payments, accounts and reports. This allows an owner to review spending without maintaining separate versions of the same information.

A practical weekly routine

  1. Review recent business spending.
  2. Record missing expenses.
  3. Check categories and descriptions.
  4. Match relevant payments to the correct records.
  5. Review unusual or duplicate transactions.
  6. Use finance reports to identify spending patterns.

Common mistakes

Frequent problems include mixing personal and business spending, leaving transactions uncategorised, recording the same expense twice and keeping receipts without linking them to a usable record.

Keep the process simple

The objective is not to create unnecessary administration. A good workflow captures enough information once and then makes that information useful across finance and reporting. OrbitBiz includes expense and finance workflows as part of its connected business workspace.
