Built around real questions

OrbitBiz is developing a knowledge layer around questions people actually ask about running a business: how to create an invoice, how to track inventory, what a purchase order does, how customer records work, how expenses should be organised and what reconciliation means. The goal is to provide useful explanations first and connect readers to relevant product features where appropriate.

How these resources are written

OrbitBiz is pre-launch, so this knowledge section does not use invented customer stories, fake usage statistics, fabricated reviews or unsupported claims. Future articles will distinguish clearly between general business guidance, current OrbitBiz functionality and planned product directions.
