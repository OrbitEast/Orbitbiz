INDIA BUSINESS GUIDE · SALONS

How Indian Salons Can Increase Revenue

Revenue can grow through better scheduling, repeat appointments, relevant add-ons and customer retention without simply extending working hours.

Make rebooking part of the service

At the end of a suitable appointment, explain when the customer may reasonably need the service again and make the next booking easy.

Offer relevant add-ons

Recommend an additional service only when it genuinely complements the customer's current appointment. Clear bundles can make choices easier while keeping the experience professional.

Reduce empty appointment slots

Track cancellations and quieter periods. Appropriate reminders and a simple waitlist can help the business use available capacity more effectively.

Sell suitable retail products

Where appropriate, recommend products connected to the service the customer received. Explain why the product is relevant instead of using aggressive selling.

Understand customer retention

Review returning customers, service history and appointment patterns. Useful records can show which services create repeat relationships and where customers stop returning.
