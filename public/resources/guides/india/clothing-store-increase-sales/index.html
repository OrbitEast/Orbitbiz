INDIA BUSINESS GUIDE · CLOTHING RETAIL

How Indian Clothing Stores Can Increase Sales

Clothing retail growth often comes from better product presentation, useful combinations, customer follow-up and disciplined stock decisions—not simply larger discounts.

Make complete looks easier to buy

Show customers how products work together. A shirt, trousers, footwear or accessory can be presented as a useful combination when the products genuinely complement one another.

Use merchandising to guide attention

Keep high-priority products visible, organised and easy to compare. Group products by use, occasion, style or customer need rather than relying only on product categories.

Protect margin instead of discounting everything

Use targeted offers for specific slow-moving products or occasions. Permanent broad discounts can train customers to wait for lower prices and may hide underlying stock problems.

Track sizes, styles and slow stock

Inventory decisions should consider variants, not only total units. A store can have enough stock overall while still missing the sizes or styles customers request most often.

Build repeat relationships

Keep appropriate customer records and follow up with relevant collections, seasonal arrivals or useful updates. Personal relevance is more valuable than sending every customer every promotion.
