SALON BUSINESS GUIDE

How to increase salon revenue without simply adding more working hours.

Salon growth can come from better use of appointments, stronger repeat visits, relevant add-ons and tighter operations.

Rebook before checkout

When a service has a natural repeat cycle, offer the customer the option to schedule the next appointment before leaving.

Reduce empty appointment slots

Review cancellations and no-shows by day and time. Use reminders and clear policies where appropriate, and contact opted-in customers when suitable slots open.

Increase appointment value

Create a small menu of complementary add-ons and recommend only what makes sense for the customer.

Add retail recommendations

Connect product recommendations to the service just completed and explain how to use the product.

Create sensible packages

Packages can make recurring services easier to plan. Calculate the economics before launch and avoid unprofitable pricing.

Reactivate inactive customers

Segment customers who have not visited within an appropriate period and send a limited number of useful, permission-based reminders.

Improve local discovery

Keep hours, contact information, services, photos and location details accurate online. Ask satisfied customers for genuine reviews.

Track service performance

Review revenue by service, staff member, time slot and product category. Look for services that consume time without producing enough value.

Protect capacity

Measure utilisation across the day and use targeted offers to fill low-demand periods instead of discounting peak periods.

Five numbers to review

Track appointments completed, no-show rate, average ticket, repeat-visit activity and retail attachment. Review them weekly and improve one area at a time.
