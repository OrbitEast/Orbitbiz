Why this resource section exists

Business software is easier to use when the underlying workflow is understandable. Orbit East is building this knowledge layer alongside OrbitBiz so users can learn what a record means, where it fits and how one business event can affect another.

The resource library will grow over time. New material should be based on useful product knowledge, genuine questions and verified information rather than filler written only to target search engines.

Current product status

OrbitBiz is still in active development and pre-launch. The resource pages therefore focus on practical concepts and the workflows the product is designed around. We do not publish invented customer stories, fake usage statistics or fabricated reviews.
