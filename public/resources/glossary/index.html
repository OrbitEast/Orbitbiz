GLOSSARY · BUSINESS TERMS

Business terms, explained clearly.

A growing reference for common terms used across sales, invoicing, purchasing, inventory, CRM and finance. Definitions are written in plain language and can be expanded as the knowledge base grows.

Invoice

A document that records a sale and the amount a customer is expected to pay for the supplied goods or services.

Receipt

A record confirming that a payment has been received, often connected to the underlying invoice or customer transaction.

Quotation

A proposed price and scope of goods or services provided to a customer before a sale is confirmed.

Purchase order

A business document used to communicate an intended purchase to a supplier, including items, quantities and agreed details.

Stock movement

A recorded change in inventory quantity, such as stock entering through a purchase or leaving through a sale.

Warehouse

A defined inventory location used to represent where products or stock are physically held.

Accounts receivable

Amounts customers still owe a business for goods or services already invoiced.

Reconciliation

The process of comparing records, such as book balances and a statement balance, to identify and resolve differences.

CRM

Customer relationship management: the practice and tools used to organise customer information, interactions, activities and sales context.

Gross margin

The amount remaining after the direct cost of goods or services is deducted from revenue, before other operating costs.

Business member

A user who has authorised access to a business workspace, subject to the application's permissions and access controls.

Business-scoped data

Data associated with a specific business so records are separated from those belonging to other businesses.
