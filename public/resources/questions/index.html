QUESTIONS

Useful answers for real workflows.

Clear answers to common questions about running connected sales, invoicing, inventory, purchasing and finance operations.

Why connect customers to invoices?

Keeping invoices attached to the correct customer preserves transaction history and makes payment follow-up, reporting and future sales easier.

What is the difference between a quotation and an invoice?

A quotation proposes terms before a sale is confirmed. An invoice records a sale and the amount due from the customer.

Why do stock movements matter?

Stock movements provide an audit trail for quantity changes. They help explain why inventory increased or decreased over time.

Why use purchase orders?

Purchase orders create a structured step between deciding to buy and receiving goods, making supplier purchasing easier to track.

What does a CRM system help organise?

A CRM can keep customer records, interactions, tasks and sales context together so follow-ups are less likely to become disconnected.

Why reconcile financial records?

Reconciliation helps identify differences between internal records and external statements so discrepancies can be investigated rather than ignored.

What makes business software connected?

Connected software lets related records move through workflows without repeatedly re-entering the same information.

What should a business report show?

A useful report should answer a business question, such as what sold, what remains unpaid, what stock needs attention or where money moved.
