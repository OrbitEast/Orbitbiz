ORBIT EAST · ORBITBIZ

Business, connected.

OrbitBiz is the business management and invoicing web app being built by Orbit East. It starts with a practical need—creating and managing invoices—and grows into connected business tools for small businesses, startups, service businesses and lean teams.

Who is building OrbitBiz?

Orbit East was founded by Nayanjyoti Ghosh. OrbitBiz is currently being built independently as a solo-maker project, with no co-founder, partner or investor at this stage.

The current focus is straightforward: build a useful product, improve it carefully, listen to genuine feedback and make business software easier for people who may not have the budget, staff or technical confidence for complicated systems.

Orbit East principle: Building the future, simply.

Why OrbitBiz exists

Many businesses need something as basic as a clear invoice, but business software can become difficult to understand, expensive to adopt or overloaded with features that a small business may never need. Spreadsheets and notebooks can also be difficult to maintain consistently as a business grows.

OrbitBiz starts from a simpler idea: make essential business work easier to access, then let the product grow with the business. A user can begin with invoicing and, when more capability is needed, move into customers, sales, inventory, purchases, payments, finance and reporting without rebuilding the same information in disconnected tools.

Our motivation

Useful business software should be accessible to people running businesses with limited resources. Basic accounting and operational record-keeping can be difficult when one owner or a small team is responsible for almost everything.

OrbitBiz is designed around that reality. Clear wording, recognisable actions, visual organisation, guidance and connected workflows should make important business tasks easier to understand. The product is currently free while it is being developed, with the goal of providing meaningful functionality rather than making essential work difficult to access.

What we are trying to solve

Invoice creation

Make an essential business document easier to create, manage and connect with customer records.

Business complexity

Bring common business records together instead of forcing users to understand several disconnected systems.

Limited resources

Help a business owner or small team manage more everyday work without needing a large administrative team.

Growing needs

Let a simple invoicing need grow into a broader connected workspace as the business becomes more organised.

Our product principles

Simple first

Users should understand the basic task before they encounter advanced workflows.

Clear controls

Simple wording, familiar icons, useful colours and guidance should help users find the right action quickly.

Connected by design

Customers, sales, purchasing, stock, payments and reporting should work as related business records.

Automation where useful

Good software should reduce repeated work while keeping important business actions understandable and controllable.

Accuracy matters

Totals, balances, permissions and business state changes must be treated seriously.

Privacy matters

Business information should stay within the appropriate business context and be visible only to authorised people.

Who we want to help

OrbitBiz is aimed especially at small shop owners, small business founders, startups, service businesses and lean teams where one owner or a small number of people may handle many responsibilities. The goal is not to assume that every business has an accountant, office staff or a technical administrator.

Privacy and trust

Business information can contain sensitive details about revenue, expenses, customers, suppliers, profits and losses. Orbit East's direction is to protect customer data and handle business information responsibly. Business records should remain within the appropriate business context and be accessible only to authorised users through the application.

We do not intend to sell or expose customer business data. Future anonymous or aggregated analytics may be considered only where they are useful, transparent and handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements. Security is an ongoing responsibility, so the product will continue to improve access controls, safeguards and data practices rather than making an unrealistic promise of zero risk.

What exists today

OrbitBiz currently has working foundations for business setup, customers, CRM activities, quotations, sales and invoicing, inventory, warehouses, purchases, purchase orders, suppliers, payments and receipts, expenses, reconciliation and reports. The product remains in active development and pre-launch refinement.

Where OrbitBiz is going

Over the next several years, the goal is to grow OrbitBiz into a broader business platform that can support both smaller businesses and larger organisations. Planned directions include deeper business intelligence, more automation, stronger accounting and tax workflows, richer CRM and procurement tools, better document and data portability, printing and document-sharing integrations, and useful connections with other services.

The product is intended to keep strong practical value available without making every useful capability expensive. In the future, a limited number of optional premium capabilities may be introduced to help sustain development. These should remain optional rather than turning essential everyday business work into an unnecessary barrier.

The wider Orbit East vision

Orbit East is intended to become a wider technology and business ecosystem, with separate products built for different needs. OrbitBiz is the first starting product, focused on invoicing and business management.

The name “Orbit” reflects the idea of connected products moving around a common centre: each product can have its own purpose while remaining part of a larger ecosystem. Over time, Orbit East may develop products or ventures across areas such as commerce, buying and selling, communication, social experiences, services, consumer products, food and other digital or physical businesses. These are long-term directions, not claims about products that exist today.

“East” reflects the company's connection to India and its starting point in the eastern side of the world, while the long-term ambition is to build products that can serve people beyond one local market.

What we will not pretend

OrbitBiz is currently pre-launch and does not have a public customer history to present as social proof. We will not publish invented user numbers, fake testimonials, fabricated reviews or unsupported performance claims. Genuine feedback, customer stories and measurable product information can be added when they actually exist.
