ORBIT EAST · ORBITBIZ

Business, connected.

OrbitBiz is the business management and invoicing web app built by Orbit East. It is being developed for small businesses, startups, service businesses and other lean teams that need practical tools without unnecessary complexity.

Why OrbitBiz exists

Many businesses need something as basic as a clear invoice, but business software can become difficult to understand, expensive to adopt or overloaded with features that a small business may never need. OrbitBiz starts from a simpler idea: make essential business work easier to access, then let the product grow with the business.

The immediate product direction is simple invoicing and connected business management. A business should be able to start with an invoice and, when it needs more, move into customers, sales, inventory, purchases, payments, finance and reporting without rebuilding the same information in separate tools.

Our motivation

We are building OrbitBiz because useful business software should be more accessible. Basic accounting and operational record-keeping can be difficult for people who do not have the budget, technical knowledge or staff to manage complicated systems. A small business should not need a large team just to keep its everyday records organised.

OrbitBiz is being designed so that an owner or a small team can handle important business information from one understandable workspace. The product is currently free while it is being developed, with a focus on meaningful functionality rather than putting essential work behind unnecessary barriers.

The principle: start with a useful task such as creating an invoice, then grow into connected business management when more capability is needed.

What we are trying to solve

Invoice creation

Make an essential business document easier to create and manage.

Business complexity

Connect common business records instead of forcing users to understand several disconnected systems.

Access to useful tools

Make practical business software easier to access for people who may not be comfortable with spreadsheets or expensive systems.

Growing business needs

Allow a simple invoicing need to grow into a broader connected workspace over time.

Our product principles

Simple first

Users should be able to understand the basic task before they encounter advanced workflows.

Clear interface

Simple wording, recognizable actions and visual organisation should help users find the right feature quickly.

Connected by design

Customers, sales, purchasing, stock, payments and reporting should work as related business records.

Accuracy matters

Business software has to treat totals, balances, permissions and state transitions seriously.

Who we want to help

OrbitBiz is aimed especially at small business owners, startups, service businesses and lean teams where one owner or a small number of people may handle many responsibilities. The product is intended to reduce the need for multiple disconnected tools and make business information easier to use.

Privacy and trust

Business information can contain sensitive details about revenue, expenses, customers, suppliers, profits and losses. Orbit East's direction is to protect customer data and handle business information responsibly. Business records should remain within the appropriate business context and be accessible only to authorised users through the application.

We do not intend to sell or expose customer business data. If anonymous or aggregated product analytics are introduced in the future, they should be implemented transparently and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements. No website can honestly promise that security risk is zero, so our approach is to keep improving safeguards, access controls and data practices.

What exists today

OrbitBiz currently has working foundations for business setup, customers, CRM activities, quotations, sales and invoicing, inventory, warehouses, purchases, purchase orders, suppliers, payments and receipts, expenses, reconciliation and reports. The product remains in active development and pre-launch refinement.

Where we want to go

Over the next several years, the goal is to grow OrbitBiz into a broader business ecosystem that can support both smaller businesses and larger organisations. Planned directions include deeper business intelligence, more automation, stronger accounting and tax workflows, richer CRM and procurement tools, better document and data portability, printing and document-sharing integrations, and useful connections with other services.

The current product is intended to provide strong practical value without making every useful capability expensive. In the future, a limited number of optional premium capabilities may be introduced to help sustain development, while the core product philosophy remains focused on accessibility and real usefulness.

The wider Orbit East vision

The name “Orbit” represents an interconnected system: different products can move around a common ecosystem while remaining useful on their own. OrbitBiz is the starting point of that direction, focused on business management and invoicing.

Over time, Orbit East may develop additional products and ventures across areas such as commerce, buying and selling, communication, social experiences, services, consumer products, food and other digital or physical businesses. These are long-term possibilities, not current products.

“East” reflects the company's connection to India and its broader ambition to build products from the eastern side of the world for users beyond one local market. The long-term ambition is to grow a technology and business ecosystem that can create useful products, new opportunities and employment as it develops.

What we will not pretend

OrbitBiz is currently pre-launch and does not have a public customer history to present as social proof. We will not publish invented user numbers, fake testimonials, fabricated reviews or unsupported performance claims. Genuine feedback and measurable product information can be added when they exist.
