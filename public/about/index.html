ORBIT EAST · ORBITBIZ

Business, connected.

OrbitBiz is the business management and invoicing web app built by Orbit East. It is being developed for small businesses, startups, service businesses and other teams that need practical tools without unnecessary complexity.

Why OrbitBiz exists

Many businesses need something as basic as a clear invoice, but business software can become difficult to understand, expensive to adopt or unnecessarily complicated. OrbitBiz starts from a simpler idea: make essential business work easier to access, then let the product grow with the business.

The immediate product direction is simple invoicing and connected business management. A business should be able to start with an invoice and, when it needs more, move into customers, sales, inventory, purchases, payments, finance and reporting without rebuilding the same information in separate tools.

Our motivation

We are building OrbitBiz because useful business software should be more accessible. Basic accounting and operational record-keeping can be difficult for people who do not have the budget, technical knowledge or time to manage complicated systems. The product is currently free while it is being developed, with a focus on making the core experience practical and easy to understand.

What we are trying to solve

Invoice creation

Make an essential business document easier to create and manage.

Business complexity

Connect common business records instead of forcing users to understand disconnected systems.

Access to useful tools

Keep the current product free and focus on practical value before adding unnecessary barriers.

Growing business needs

Allow a simple invoicing need to grow into a broader connected workspace over time.

Our product principles

Simple first

Users should be able to understand the basic task before they encounter advanced workflows.

Connected by design

Customers, sales, purchasing, stock, payments and reporting should work as related business records.

Clarity over clutter

The interface should make important information easier to find and everyday actions easier to understand.

Accuracy matters

Business software has to treat totals, balances, permissions and state transitions seriously.

What exists today

OrbitBiz currently has working foundations for business setup, customers, CRM activities, quotations, sales and invoicing, inventory, warehouses, purchases, purchase orders, suppliers, payments and receipts, expenses, reconciliation and reports. The product remains in active development and pre-launch refinement.

Where we want to go

Over the next several years, the goal is to grow OrbitBiz into a broader business ecosystem that can support both smaller businesses and larger organisations. Planned directions include deeper business intelligence, more automation, stronger accounting and tax workflows, richer CRM and procurement tools, better document and data portability, printing and document-sharing integrations, and useful connections with other services. These are future directions, not claims that every capability is already available.

The wider Orbit East vision

The name “Orbit” represents an interconnected system: different products and services can move around a common ecosystem while remaining useful on their own. Orbit East is intended to become a broader digital product ecosystem over time, potentially spanning business tools, commerce, communication and other practical digital services.

“East” reflects the company's connection to India and its broader ambition to build products from the eastern side of the world for users beyond one local market.

Privacy and trust

Orbit East's direction is to protect customer data and handle business information responsibly. We do not intend to sell or expose customer business data. If anonymous or aggregated product analytics are introduced in the future, they should be implemented transparently and in accordance with the applicable privacy requirements. No website can honestly promise that security risk is zero, so our approach is to keep improving safeguards, access controls and data practices.

What we will not pretend

OrbitBiz is currently pre-launch and does not have a public customer history to present as social proof. We will not publish invented user numbers, fake testimonials, fabricated reviews or unsupported performance claims. Genuine feedback and measurable product information can be added when they exist.
