ORBIT EAST · ORBITBIZ

Business, connected.

OrbitBiz is the business management and invoice web app built by Orbit East. It is being developed as a connected workspace for customers, CRM, sales, quotations, invoices, inventory, purchases, payments, finance and reports.

Why we are building OrbitBiz

The idea is straightforward: everyday business work should not require repeatedly moving the same information between disconnected tools. A customer, sale, invoice, payment, purchase or stock movement can affect other parts of a business. OrbitBiz is being built around that connection.

Our motivation

Our motivation is to make useful business software more understandable and practical. Instead of adding complexity for its own sake, the product direction is to connect the records a business already needs and make the relationship between them clear.

Our goal

The goal is to build a dependable web-based business workspace that can grow with real operational needs. That means getting the fundamentals right first: data relationships, calculations, business workflows, access control, reliability and a usable interface.

Connected by design

Customers, sales, purchasing, stock, payments and reporting should work as one system.

Built around real workflows

Records and status changes should represent actual business operations rather than disconnected demo screens.

Clarity over clutter

The interface should make important information easier to find and everyday actions easier to understand.

Trust through accuracy

Business software has to treat totals, balances, permissions and state transitions seriously.

What exists today

OrbitBiz currently has working foundations for business setup, customers, CRM activities, quotations, sales and invoicing, inventory, warehouses, purchases, purchase orders, suppliers, payments and receipts, expenses, reconciliation and reports. The product is still in active development and is not presented as a finished platform.

Where we want to go

The long-term direction is to deepen reporting and business intelligence, improve automation, expand accounting and tax workflows, strengthen multi-warehouse operations, add richer CRM and procurement capabilities, improve document and data portability, and develop useful integrations. These are planned directions, not claims that all of these capabilities are already available.

What we will not pretend

OrbitBiz is currently pre-launch and does not have a public user base or customer feedback history to present as social proof. We will not publish invented user numbers, fake testimonials, fabricated reviews or unsupported performance claims. When genuine feedback and measurable product information become available, they can be added transparently.

About Orbit East

Orbit East is the product identity behind OrbitBiz. The broader direction is to build focused digital products under a consistent ecosystem rather than treating every tool as an isolated project.
