ORBIT EAST · ORBITBIZ

Business, connected.

OrbitBiz is the business management and invoice web app built by Orbit East. It brings everyday business operations into one connected workspace instead of scattering customers, sales, stock, purchases, payments and reports across disconnected tools.

What OrbitBiz is

OrbitBiz is designed as a practical web workspace for businesses that need to manage operational records and follow how one business event affects another. Customers can connect to quotations and invoices; purchases can connect to suppliers and stock; payments and receipts can feed financial records and reports.

Product direction

Connected

Business records should work together instead of being isolated screens.

Accurate

Totals, balances and status changes should reflect real business workflows.

Practical

The interface should make everyday work easier to understand and complete.

Progressive

The product can grow from core operations into deeper reporting, automation and integrations.

What it covers

  • Customers and CRM
  • Sales, quotations and invoicing
  • Inventory and warehouses
  • Purchases and suppliers
  • Payments, receipts and finance
  • Reports and business insights

About Orbit East

Orbit East is the product identity behind OrbitBiz. The long-term direction is to build useful, focused digital tools under one consistent product ecosystem, with OrbitBiz serving as the connected business-management workspace.

Built for the web

OrbitBiz is delivered as a web application so the workspace can evolve across desktop and mobile experiences without requiring a traditional desktop installation.
