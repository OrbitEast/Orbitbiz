PRIVACY · CURRENT INFORMATION

Privacy is part of the product.

OrbitBiz is designed to handle business information through authenticated, business-scoped application workflows. Because the service is still pre-launch, this page describes the current scope and identifies the areas that will be covered by the formal public privacy policy.

Information areas the policy covers

  • Account and authentication information
  • Business, customer and transaction data entered into the application
  • How application data is stored and processed
  • Security and access controls
  • Data retention, deletion and account handling
  • User rights and privacy contact procedures

Data minimisation and accuracy

OrbitBiz should only publish privacy statements that match the actual product. The privacy page will be expanded as the public service, data flows and operational requirements are finalised. No invented third-party data practices are stated here.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the official Orbit East support address: support.orbiteast@gmail.com.
