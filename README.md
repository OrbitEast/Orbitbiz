# OrbitBiz

### Business, connected.

**OrbitBiz** is a modern business management and invoicing platform by **Orbit East**, designed to bring everyday business operations into one connected workspace.

Instead of keeping customers, sales, invoices, stock, purchases and payments in separate places, OrbitBiz keeps them connected so business information can move naturally from one step to the next.

**Live:** https://orbitbiz.web.app/

---

## What is OrbitBiz?

OrbitBiz is built for businesses that want a simpler way to manage day-to-day operations from the web.

It brings together:

- Customers and customer records
- CRM and follow-ups
- Sales and quotations
- Invoices and payments
- Products and inventory
- Purchases and suppliers
- Purchase orders
- Warehouses and stock
- Expenses and finance
- Reports and business activity

The goal is straightforward: **less scattered information, fewer repeated entries, and a clearer view of the business.**

---

## One connected workflow

A business rarely works in isolated steps. OrbitBiz is designed around that reality.

**Sales**

Customer → Quotation → Invoice → Payment → Customer history

**Purchasing**

Supplier → Purchase Order → Purchase → Stock → Supplier payment

Because these areas are connected, information entered in one part of the business can support the next step instead of being entered again.

---

## Key features

### Sales & Invoicing
Create quotations and invoices, manage sales activity and keep payment information connected to customers.

### Customers & CRM
Keep customer details, activities, follow-ups and business history together.

### Inventory
Manage products, stock movements and warehouse information while keeping inventory connected with sales and purchasing.

### Purchases & Suppliers
Manage suppliers, purchase orders, receiving and supplier payments in one workflow.

### Finance
Track expenses, payments, receipts and reconciliation activity with the rest of the business context available.

### Reports
Get a clearer view of sales, financial activity, inventory and other important business operations.

## Privacy & security

Business information should stay within the business context where it belongs.

OrbitBiz uses authenticated access, business-level permissions, database security policies and validation for important business operations. Critical workflows are designed to protect relationships between records such as invoices, payments, purchases and stock.

Security and reliability will continue to evolve as the product grows.
